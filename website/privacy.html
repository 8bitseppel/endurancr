3. The endurancr app

endurancr is designed so that we never receive your data. The app has no user account, no server or backend, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We, the developer, have no access to any of the data described below.

  • Training plan and settings. Your goal, plan and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Apple Health. With your permission, the app reads workouts, heart rate and workout routes from Apple Health and saves your runs to Apple Health as standard workouts. This data never leaves your device through endurancr. It only leaves your device through Apple's own iCloud and Health sync, if you have enabled it, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in the Health app or in the iOS Settings.
  • Location. With your permission, location is used only while you are recording a run, to measure distance and to record the route. It is not used in the background outside of a run and is not sent anywhere.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch sync. Your plan and runs are synced directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. No server of ours is involved.
  • Backups and exports. A backup or export file is only created when you explicitly choose to create and share it, and it goes only where you send it, for example to the Files app or to another app of your choice.

Deleting the app removes the plan and settings stored by endurancr from your device. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Where the app processes data on your device on the basis of your permission, the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR for health data). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR) by revoking the permission in the iOS Settings or the Health app.

Apple processes the data needed for this, such as your Apple Account and device information, as an independent controller. We use this information only to fix bugs and improve the app. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a stable app. For details see the Apple privacy policy and the TestFlight terms.

6. Your rights (Betroffenenrechte)

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • the right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • the right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • the right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • the right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object (Widerspruchsrecht, Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

To exercise your rights, simply write to info@kaessinger.com. Please note that we do not hold any app data about you, so for data inside the app or in Apple Health you can view, export and delete it yourself on your device at any time.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
