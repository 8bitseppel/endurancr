Completely fresh

Nothing from before: no track, no e, no road, no dot, no sun, no spiral, no shield. Eight new things a runner knows.

D1

Sweat drop

One big drop of sweat, leaning into the wind. Endurance is effort, and this is what it looks like.

D2

The laurel

The marathon was born in Greece, and the winner got a laurel wreath. Every finish in the app is yours to crown.

D3

Tie your laces

A shoelace bow, the last thing you do before every run.

D4

8-bit sneaker

A running shoe in pixels, speeding off like in an old arcade game. Playful, and a wink at 8bitseppel.

D5

Cadence

Left, right, left, right: your steps as a rhythm, left foot up and right foot down, building to the middle of the run.

D6

One wing

Hermes, the messenger with wings on his feet. Just one wing, swept back by speed.

D7

Split time

A stopwatch, most of the way round. The filled part is the plan you have already done.

D8

Finisher medal

The medal at the end of the race, still on its ribbon. The goal the whole app is built around.
