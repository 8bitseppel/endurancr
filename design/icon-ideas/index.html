Ten new icon ideas

What I took from Apple's guidance and from icons that read well at a glance (Strava, Nike Run Club, Apple Fitness, Workout, Headspace):

  • One bold shape that fills most of the square. The home screen shows it at 60 points, Settings at 29.
  • Thick strokes only. Thin lines vanish at small sizes, which is why today's icon looks small.
  • It should still be recognisable blurred, in dark, in tinted grey, and inside the watch circle.
  • No text, no baked-in shadows or gloss: iOS 26 adds the glass itself.

Each card shows those tests under the large icon. Further down, every idea sits among other apps on a home screen.

N1

The track

A running track seen from above, filling the width. You are the dot in the infield.

N2

The window

One big round window, and through it a sun rising over water.

N3

Bold sunrise

A solid half sun on the horizon, nothing cut out of it, one thick lane around it, the water below in three strokes.

N4

The bend

Three lanes sweep round the bend from the corner. The dot is the sun ahead of you.

N5

The e

The first letter, drawn like a lap: the bar is the finish line and the dot is you, just coming out of it.

N6

The loop

Your route as one thick loop, half run, half ahead. The dot in the middle is you.

N7

The sun, on the water

One big disc, nothing else, its lower part broken by three lines of water. Bold like Headspace.

N8

The dial

A thick ring, filled most of the way round, like a week that is going well. You are the dot at the centre.

N9

Mirror, bold

Today's icon rebuilt thick: one heavy lane, its reflection, and the sun as a solid dot.

N10

Striped sun

A full sun, cut into lines that thin out as it sinks, like lanes seen from the stand.
