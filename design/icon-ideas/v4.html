Going crazy

No safe choices this time: a louder electric violet, and ideas no other running app uses. Running is still the main thing in every one.

C1

Fingerprint

Running: Six laps of a running track, nested inside each other.
Only for you: Together they make a fingerprint. Nobody else has yours: your runs are yours alone.

C2

The 42 km spiral

Running: The whole marathon wound into one line. You start on the outside.
Only for you: The finish is at the heart of it, where nothing reaches. Hypnotic on purpose.

C3

Long exposure

Running: A photo with the shutter open: you, the dot, getting bigger and brighter with every stride.
Only for you: No privacy mark. It just feels like one person, alone, moving.

C4

Your own map

Running: Contour lines of a hill, and your route climbing it to the top.
Only for you: A map that only you have, drawn by your own feet.

C5

Through the wall

Running: Kilometre 32, the wall every marathon runner knows. You go straight through it.
Only for you: The wall stays standing all around: you are through, it keeps everyone else out.

C6

Breaking the tape

Running: The finish line, the moment the tape snaps. You are the dot going through.
Only for you: No privacy mark: it is the one moment every runner wants.

C7

The race sash

Running: The diagonal sash on a racing vest, filling the whole icon edge to edge. Loud like a kit.
Only for you: No mark. Wearing it is a statement about you, not about data.

C8

The race bib

Running: A race number pinned on, tilted like it is on a moving runner, with an e instead of a number.
Only for you: A bib is personal: it is yours, with your name on the list. Quiet ownership, no lock needed.
