Running first, only for you second

The strongest running shapes from the last round, each with the privacy idea added back as a detail: small, inside the running shape, never the main thing. Three ways of saying it: you are held inside the shape (B1, B3, B6), a small shield or house takes a detail's place (B2, B4, B8), or the run ends at home (B5, B7).

B1

The forward e, you inside

Running: The e leans forward and runs on out of the frame.
Only for you: You, the dot, sit safe inside the letter, sheltered by it.

B2

The forward e, shield inside

Running: The same forward e.
Only for you: The space inside the e holds a small shield, the size of a detail.

B3

The track around you

Running: A running track, one whole lap.
Only for you: You are the dot in the infield: the track closes all the way round you, nothing gets in or out.

B4

On the track, shield as runner

Running: A 400 m track, most of the lap done.
Only for you: The runner on the bend is a small shield instead of a dot.

B5

The road home

Running: A road running to the horizon.
Only for you: Where the sun would rise there is a house: every run ends at home, on your phone.

B6

The e as lanes, you inside

Running: The e drawn as two lanes of a track bend.
Only for you: You, the dot, in the middle of the lanes, wrapped by them.

B7

The loop back home

Running: Your route as one bold loop.
Only for you: It starts and finishes at a small house: the run comes back home with you, and stays there.

B8

On the move, shield at the core

Running: You, the dot, with the lanes streaming behind.
Only for you: Inside the dot, a small shield: protection is at your core, not on show.
