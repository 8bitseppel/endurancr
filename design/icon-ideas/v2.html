Running first

This time I looked up the real App Store icons of 25 running and privacy apps. What I learned:

Strava, COROS, RunnaThe ones that read best: one bold mark filling about 60 to 75% of the square, on one strong colour.
Pacer, Garmin ConnectThe ones that read worst: several objects, text, badges and 3D.
Nike Run Club, adidasUse words, which only works with a famous brand.
Apple Workout, WorkOutDoorsAlready use the running figure, and Apple Fitness has the rings. Both are taken.
Signal, Proton, DuckDuckGoPrivacy apps that show no lock or shield at all. They earn trust with calm colour and simple shapes.
ColourRunning apps use orange, red, blue, green and teal. Purple is free.

So these ideas are about running only, on a deep purple background (Apple advises against a pale one), with one bold shape and nothing that belongs to someone else. Apple's rules: at most four layers, no text, no shadows or gloss (iOS adds the glass), rounded bold shapes, and it must survive the watch circle. Privacy is left to the colour and the words in the App Store. Only A3 hints at it.

A1

The forward e

The first letter, leaning forward like a runner, its tail running on out of the frame. The way Runna and Strava use one bold letter or mark.

A2

The long road

A road that bends away to the horizon, the sun where it ends. Endurance is the distance still ahead.

A3

On the track

A 400 m track, most of the lap done, you on the bend. The loop closes on itself, a quiet hint that nothing leaves it.

A4

Getting fitter

Two curves rising, last block and this one, the bold one pulling ahead. Progress over weeks, not a single run.

A5

The e as lanes

The e drawn as two lanes of a track bend, the bar across it like a finish line.

A6

On the move

You, the dot, with the lanes streaming behind. Pure speed, nothing else.

A7

Building up

Three glassy bars leaning forward, each taller: weeks of training stacking up. The layered look Proton uses, turned into running.

A8

Endless loop

A route drawn as an infinity loop: endurance, run after run. Half of it is done in bold.
