# Security

endurancr has no server, no account and no analytics. Your training plan stays on
your device, and your runs live in Apple Health. There is no backend to attack,
but bugs in the app can still matter, for example in how backups are imported.

If you find a security or privacy issue, please do not open a public issue.
Email info@kaessinger.com instead, with steps to reproduce. You will get a reply
within a week.
